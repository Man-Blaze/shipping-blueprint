About Us

Example Shipping Co has served Vanuatu's islands for over 20 years. We operate landing craft (LC) vessels providing transport for passengers, cargo, and outer-island relief supplies.

We are committed to safe, reliable, and affordable shipping for every community in Vanuatu — from Port Vila to the most remote islands.
